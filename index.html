Why we built it

Medication non-adherence kills 125,000 Americans every year.

It costs the US healthcare system over $300 billion annually. It is one of the largest preventable causes of death in the country. We're two physicians who watched it happen to patients we cared about. We built PillTime to remove the friction that turns "I'll do it later" into "I forgot."

What's in the app

A pocket pharmacist that respects your privacy.

📷

Multi-angle bottle scanner

Curved-label aware. Takes 1–3 photos around the bottle, then asks the AI to reconcile the wrap-around text.

🎤

Say med + dose

No camera? No problem. Tap the mic, speak the medication name and dosage out loud, and AI extracts everything — forgiving of mispronunciations.

🛡️

Zero PHI by design

The scanner is instructed never to extract your name, doctor's name, or Rx number — and we strip them on-device as a backup.

🆓

Free AI by default

Defaults to Google Gemini's free tier. No credit card required to scan bottles.

⏰

Smart reminders

Dose, refill (~7 days before empty), and end-of-course local notifications.

⌚

Apple Watch ready

Tap ✓ Taken, Snooze 10 min, or Skip right from your wrist — without unlocking your phone.

🔊

Hear it pronounced

Tap the speaker to hear any drug name spoken aloud with the right syllable stressed.

📞

Tap to call your pharmacy

Phone and address parsed from the bottle. One tap calls, one tap opens Maps.

💸

Find savings on every drug

Direct links to GoodRx, SingleCare, NeedyMeds, RxAssist, Medicare Plan Finder. No PillTime commission.

📚

Real research, not invented

Links to NIH MedlinePlus, FDA DailyMed, PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, Drugs.com.

📋

Doctor visit summary

One tap shares a clean, PHI-free list of every med you take — plus your adherence numbers.

🔒 No backend. No analytics. No PHI.

Your medications, schedule, and history live only on your device. There is no PillTime server that receives or stores any of it. No ad SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no user accounts. Delete the app to delete everything.
