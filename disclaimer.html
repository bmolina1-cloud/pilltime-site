⚠️ PILLTIME IS NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE AND NOT MEDICAL ADVICE.

PillTime is a personal medication-tracking and reminder tool. It is not a medical device under U.S. Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act §201(h). It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

Information sources can be incomplete or wrong

The information PillTime shows about your medications — including drug names parsed from a bottle photo, dosage, frequency, indications, warnings, side effects, and interactions — is sourced from public databases (FDA, NIH, RxNorm) or generated by a third-party AI vision model. It is provided for general informational purposes only and may be incomplete, outdated, or wrong.

AI vision has limits

The AI label-reader can and does make mistakes, especially on curved, glossy, faded, partially obscured, or non-English labels. You are solely responsible for verifying every field — drug name, strength, frequency, schedule — against the printed label and your actual prescription before relying on it.

Reminders can fail

PillTime uses your device's local notification system. Delivery depends on your operating system, battery, do-not-disturb settings, and a dozen other factors outside our control. PillTime cannot guarantee that any reminder will fire, on time or at all. Do not use PillTime as your only safeguard against a missed dose, especially for time-critical medications.

Always follow your label and your prescriber

Always follow your prescription label and the instructions of your licensed healthcare provider. Never start, stop, or change a medication based on what PillTime tells you.

Emergency contacts

  • In a medical emergency, call 911 (US) or your local emergency number immediately.
  • For poison or overdose concerns in the U.S., contact Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222.
  • Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US): 988.
